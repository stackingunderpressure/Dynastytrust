## 1. What DynastyTrust is

DynastyTrust is a non-custodial Bitcoin coordination tool for
creating Taproot multisig vaults with inheritance, recovery, and
governance rules. The service is operated by DynastyTrust
(hereafter "we" or "the service") and consists of:

- A web application that helps you assemble vaults
- Netlify-hosted serverless functions that coordinate metadata
- A Rust compiler service that converts policy into descriptors
- A database that stores vault metadata, member roster, proposals,
  attestations, and events
- Optional legal and tax referrals through third-party firms

The service does NOT include custody of your keys, your coins,
or any financial obligations to you.
